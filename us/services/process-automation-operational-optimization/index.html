Business Systems Engineering service

Process Automation & Operational Optimization

For processes that are slow, manual, invisible or sustained by people who bridge gaps between systems.

Discuss a system challenge
01 · Problems

When this capability matters

  • 01Rekeying and manual data entry connect systems.
  • 02Email has become the workflow engine.
  • 03Owners and escalation paths are unclear.
  • 04Automating one step creates a problem in another.
  • 05There is no useful starting baseline.
  • 06Exceptions were not designed.
  • 07Status labels do not match real execution.
  • 08Rule changes make automation fragile.
02 · MTera

How MTera works across the system

01

Map real work rather than only the prescribed process.

02

Identify waiting, repetition, exceptions and manual controls.

03

Decide what to automate, simplify or leave deliberately human.

04

Design workflow, rules, authority and escalation together.

05

Integrate with existing systems around clear contracts.

06

Establish a meaningful baseline and measurement plan.

07

Design operational monitoring and improvement ownership.

03 · Deliverables

A target state that can be acted on

  • Current-state process map
  • Friction and waste analysis
  • Automation opportunity matrix
  • Target workflow
  • Rule and exception model
  • Integration specification
  • KPI baseline and measurement plan
  • Phased implementation roadmap
Outcome

A more visible and resilient flow of work in which automation supports the business outcome while preserving exception handling and accountable human decisions.

Automation is not assumed to be the answer to every step. Simplification, removal or an explicit human control may be the better design.
