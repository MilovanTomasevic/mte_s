Business Systems Engineering service

Custom Software & Platform Engineering

For situations in which no standard product solves the specific business problem, or an existing system needs a precise extension, integration or new internal capability.

Discuss a system challenge
01 · Problems

When this capability matters

  • 01The requirement is too specific for a standard tool.
  • 02Several systems must behave as one business capability.
  • 03A prototype is not ready for production.
  • 04The existing solution is difficult to maintain.
  • 05Architecture decisions are undocumented.
  • 06Delivery lacks sufficient business context.
  • 07Technical debt blocks necessary change.
02 · MTera

How MTera works across the system

01

Frame the problem, boundaries and build-or-buy decision.

02

Run product or service discovery around real operations.

03

Define architecture and explicit technical choices.

04

Engineer the components that carry distinctive value.

05

Integrate data and surrounding systems.

06

Design operability, testing and security into delivery.

07

Document decisions and transfer knowledge.

08

Plan maintenance, transition and future evolution.

03 · Deliverables

A target state that can be acted on

  • Solution brief
  • Architecture
  • Delivery plan
  • Proof of concept when justified
  • Production-grade implementation
  • Integration components
  • Test and deployment pipeline
  • Operational documentation
  • Transition and handoff plan
Outcome

A maintainable capability engineered around a specific business need, with its decisions, boundaries and operating responsibilities made explicit.

This is not low-cost software outsourcing. The focus is a specific, complex systems problem and senior accountability for the engineering path.
