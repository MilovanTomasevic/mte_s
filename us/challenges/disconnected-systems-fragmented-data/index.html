System challenge

When critical work is split across systems, teams and versions of truth

Disconnection is rarely just an interface gap. It appears when identity, meaning, status and responsibility change as work moves between systems. MTera maps the end-to-end business interaction, identifies where continuity is lost and engineers a target state in which data and execution remain explainable across the whole flow.

Bring one broken handoff or disputed status to a system assessment.
01

What you may be seeing

  • Teams maintain parallel records for the same business object.
  • People copy data between applications or reconcile it in spreadsheets.
  • Status differs depending on which system or team is asked.
  • Errors are found late and ownership is disputed.
  • Decisions wait for manual consolidation.
  • A local change creates unexpected work elsewhere.
02

Why partial fixes fail

Adding another interface or dashboard treats the visible symptom. Without shared identity, semantics, ownership and recovery rules, the new layer can reproduce the fragmentation behind a cleaner screen.

03

Business consequence

Decisions slow down, operational effort moves into invisible reconciliation and customers experience failures that no single team can diagnose.

04 · Target state

Critical entities have controlled identities and definitions; exchanges carry meaningful status; ownership is explicit; exceptions can be traced, resolved and verified from source to outcome.

05

Approach

  1. 01Frame the business decisions and outcomes affected by fragmentation.
  2. 02Map people, data, systems, interfaces and manual bridges together.
  3. 03Identify semantic, ownership and control breaks.
  4. 04Define the smallest coherent target architecture and transition states.
  5. 05Sequence data, integration and process changes around operational risk.
06

Key decisions

  1. 01Which source is authoritative for each critical fact?
  2. 02Where should identity be created and preserved?
  3. 03Which status and error states must cross boundaries?
  4. 04What may remain decentralized without losing coherence?
07

Deliverables

  1. 01End-to-end system and data-flow map
  2. 02Critical entity and ownership model
  3. 03Integration and data contract requirements
  4. 04Target and transition architecture
  5. 05Prioritized remediation roadmap
