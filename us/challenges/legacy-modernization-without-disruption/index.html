System challenge

Modernize a legacy system without losing the business it quietly holds together

A legacy system contains more than old technology: it often carries undocumented rules, operational memory and dependencies that keep the business running. MTera makes that context visible, defines safe transition states and engineers staged change with explicit coexistence, verification, recovery and ownership decisions.

Start by identifying the capability that cannot tolerate a blind cutover.
01

What you may be seeing

  • Replacement scope keeps growing as hidden dependencies emerge.
  • Business rules exist only in code or experienced people's routines.
  • A single cutover would place continuity at unacceptable risk.
  • Old and new systems must operate together, but coexistence is undefined.
  • Data migration focuses on fields while meaning and history are uncertain.
  • Teams defer necessary change because the blast radius is unknown.
02

Why partial fixes fail

A technology replacement plan assumes the current system is only an application. Rebuilding screens and tables does not preserve tacit rules, decision authority, timing dependencies or recovery paths.

03

Business consequence

Programs overrun, operations carry parallel manual controls and a forced cutover can interrupt revenue, service or regulatory evidence.

04 · Target state

The target is reached through testable transition states. Business rules and data meaning are explicit, coexistence is designed, each migration step has acceptance evidence and recovery is possible.

05

Approach

  1. 01Discover the actual business capabilities and rules in the legacy system landscape.
  2. 02Map dependencies, timing, data lineage and operational workarounds.
  3. 03Separate stable business truth from replaceable implementation.
  4. 04Define target and transition architectures with coexistence boundaries.
  5. 05Plan increments, verification and rollback around material risk.
06

Key decisions

  1. 01Which capabilities should be replaced, wrapped, extracted or retired?
  2. 02What business truth must be preserved through every transition?
  3. 03How will old and new states be reconciled during coexistence?
  4. 04What evidence permits progression to the next stage?
07

Deliverables

  1. 01Legacy capability and dependency map
  2. 02Business rule and data continuity register
  3. 03Target and transition architecture
  4. 04Migration, coexistence and recovery plan
  5. 05Decision-led modernization roadmap
