System challenge

Expose the work people perform between systems before automating it

Manual work is often the control layer that makes incomplete systems function. MTera maps the real flow, including waiting, judgment, exceptions and informal reconciliation, then separates valuable human decisions from avoidable friction and engineers automation that preserves context and responsibility.

Bring the manual step everyone depends on but no system acknowledges.
01

What you may be seeing

  • Email and spreadsheets carry status between formal tools.
  • Experienced people know exceptions that the process map omits.
  • Work waits because authority and escalation are unclear.
  • The same data is entered repeatedly and corrected downstream.
  • Existing automation fails whenever a rule or exception changes.
  • Management sees completion counts but not the effort spent on blocked or reworked cases.
02

Why partial fixes fail

Automating the documented sequence ignores the human controls and exceptions that keep it safe. Automating repeated steps can make a brittle process faster while moving errors and effort to a less visible point.

03

Business consequence

Capacity is consumed by reconciliation, service depends on individual memory and changes produce unpredictable operational failures.

04 · Target state

Work, status and ownership are visible; rules and exceptions are explicit; automation handles repeatable execution while accountable people retain meaningful judgment and escalation paths.

05

Approach

  1. 01Observe and map actual work across people and systems.
  2. 02Quantify waiting and rework only where a defensible baseline exists.
  3. 03Identify hidden decisions, controls and exception classes.
  4. 04Simplify before selecting automation.
  5. 05Design integrations, authority, monitoring and improvement ownership.
06

Key decisions

  1. 01Which work adds judgment and which only bridges a system gap?
  2. 02Which exceptions can be classified and which need human discretion?
  3. 03Where must authority and evidence remain visible?
  4. 04What baseline will show whether the change helps?
07

Deliverables

  1. 01Actual-work process map
  2. 02Friction, control and exception analysis
  3. 03Automation opportunity matrix
  4. 04Target workflow and integration design
  5. 05Measurement and phased delivery plan
