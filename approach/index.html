Business Systems Engineering

From a system challenge to an engineered result

MTera moves from the business decision to a whole-system understanding, an explicit target, an engineered path and delivery evidence that improves the next cycle.

01

Five connected phases

The phases create a visible line from reason to result without pretending that discovery ends when delivery starts.

02

Four distinct ways to engage

Scope follows the question, available evidence and decision risk. Fixed prices or durations are not published until a commercial model and concrete scope are approved.

03

Work with the people who own the system

MTera can work alongside internal teams and existing suppliers. Responsibilities, decisions, artefacts and knowledge transfer are made explicit so the organisation retains operational control.

Engagement models

Four distinct ways to engage

Scope follows the question, available evidence and decision risk. Fixed prices or durations are not published until a commercial model and concrete scope are approved.

01

System Assessment

A bounded assessment that answers one consequential system question with evidence, risks and a recommended direction.

When
A decision is blocked by an incomplete view of the current system, competing explanations or uncertain dependencies.
Input
  • A clearly framed question
  • Access to relevant people and artefacts
  • Known constraints and prior decisions
Output
  • Evidence-led findings
  • Risk and constraint view
  • Prioritised recommendation
  • Decision brief
Duration
Duration is set after the question, access and evidence boundary are agreed; no standard duration is implied.
Use the recommendation to decide whether to stop, investigate further, define architecture or begin delivery.
02

Architecture & Roadmap

Target and transition architecture, decisions, sequencing, ownership and acceptance criteria for a defined change.

When
The challenge is understood but the organisation needs a coherent target state and a safe, decision-led path to reach it.
Input
  • Confirmed business objective
  • Current-state evidence
  • Material constraints
  • Available decision-makers
Output
  • Target and transition architecture
  • Decision records
  • Prioritised roadmap
  • Governance and acceptance model
Duration
Scope follows the number of capabilities, boundaries, decisions and transition risks that must be resolved.
Mobilise delivery with explicit ownership and use the architecture as a living decision system.
03

Engineering Delivery

Hands-on design, implementation, integration and technical leadership for the parts of change that require accountable engineering.

When
A target direction exists and the organisation needs senior technical work that stays connected to business purpose and operations.
Input
  • Prioritised outcome
  • Architecture or an agreed discovery boundary
  • Delivery environment and team interfaces
Output
  • Working, tested capability
  • Integrated components
  • Operational documentation
  • Decision history and knowledge transfer
Duration
Delivery is staged around useful increments, risk and acceptance evidence rather than a pre-set package length.
Transfer accountable operation, measure the result and feed learning into the next MTera cycle.
04

Strategic Technical Advisory

Periodic senior support for leaders making consequential architecture, data, integration, AI and transformation decisions.

When
The organisation owns delivery but benefits from an independent systems view at key decision points.
Input
  • Decision agenda
  • Relevant evidence before each session
  • Access to accountable leaders
Output
  • Decision challenge and advice
  • Recorded rationale
  • Risk and dependency observations
  • Recommended next evidence
Duration
Cadence and review points are agreed around the organisation's decision cycle and may be ended when no longer useful.
Reassess the advisory agenda regularly and convert only defined needs into assessment, architecture or delivery work.
