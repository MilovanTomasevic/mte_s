System challenge

Turn an AI pilot into an accountable operational capability

A successful demonstration proves technical possibility, not operational readiness. MTera connects the use case to a real workflow, clarifies data fitness and human authority, defines evaluation and monitoring, and designs the architecture, fallback and ownership required for controlled production use.

Start with the decision, failure boundary and owner behind the pilot.
01

What you may be seeing

  • The pilot has attention but no accountable business owner.
  • Evaluation is based on impressive examples rather than acceptance criteria.
  • Production data and workflow differ from the demonstration.
  • Human review exists as an idea but not an operating design.
  • Cost, latency, monitoring and fallback are unknown.
  • The team cannot state when the capability should stop or escalate.
02

Why partial fixes fail

Deploying the model behind an endpoint only changes its location. It does not establish decision rights, reliable data flows, human oversight, operating support or evidence that behaviour remains acceptable.

03

Business consequence

The pilot stalls indefinitely or enters use with unclear authority, uncontrolled risk and no sustainable way to detect or correct failure.

04 · Target state

The capability has an owner, bounded purpose, fit-for-use data, explicit human and model roles, measurable acceptance criteria, monitoring, fallback, incident response and an understood operating cost.

05

Approach

  1. 01Reframe the pilot around a business decision and responsible owner.
  2. 02Assess data, workflow, architecture and operating readiness.
  3. 03Define human oversight, autonomy boundaries and fallback.
  4. 04Engineer evaluation, monitoring and evidence into the workflow.
  5. 05Sequence productionisation and adoption through controlled stages.
06

Key decisions

  1. 01What may the model recommend, decide or execute?
  2. 02Which failures require human intervention or service suspension?
  3. 03How will quality be measured under real operating conditions?
  4. 04What exit options exist for model or supplier change?
07

Deliverables

  1. 01Production readiness assessment
  2. 02AI integration and oversight architecture
  3. 03Evaluation and monitoring plan
  4. 04Operating, fallback and incident model
  5. 05Staged productionisation roadmap
